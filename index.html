ADVANCED ARMORY

HOW TO PLAY (STAGE 5)

5 PARTS PER WEAPON: Assemble and maintain standard weapon configurations.

INSPECTION: Deep dirt and bore blockages are invisible. Drag the INSPECT (i) lens over parts to zoom in and reveal hidden brown dirt spots or blockages.

CLEANING PROTOCOL (Strict Order):
1. GRIME: Dip the CLOTH in the WATER BOWL, then scrub off brown surface rust/grime.
2. DEEP DIRT: If the part is METAL, use the STEEL PICK. If it's NON-METAL (Wood/Polymer), scrub with the BRUSH.
3. BORE BLOCKAGE: If the part is a BARREL, you must clear the inside by sliding the BRASS ROD into it.

SCORING: S (1000pts), A (900pts), B (750pts), C (600pts), D (300pts), F (50pts).
